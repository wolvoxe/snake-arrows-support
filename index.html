Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove ads?

Go to the "Remove Ads" section inside the app and complete the purchase to enjoy an ad-free experience instantly.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings menu to restore your previous purchase using the same Apple ID / Google account.
